# Page Plan Format

Use this compact example when planning before image generation.

```text
Expansion mode: Slight Expansion
Maximum pages: 5

Page 1
Template: generated-layout-5-panel-balanced.png
Page purpose: Establish the protagonist, setting, and first problem.
Continuity: Protagonist wears the same school jacket from the reference image; rainy evening street; anxious mood.

Panel 1:
Draw: Wide establishing shot of the rainy shopping street, protagonist small in frame under a convenience store awning.
Dialogue: none
SFX: "ザアア"
Balloon/caption placement: Small SFX along the rain at upper left.

Panel 2:
Draw: Close-up of the protagonist noticing a torn poster on the wall.
Dialogue: "This mark..."
SFX: none
Balloon/caption placement: Small bubble near the face, not covering the eyes.

Panel 3:
Draw: Insert shot of the torn poster with a mysterious symbol and a missing-person notice.
Dialogue: none
SFX: "ピタ"
Balloon/caption placement: SFX beside the hand stopping on the poster edge.

Panel 4:
Draw: Over-the-shoulder view as a shadow appears reflected in the wet pavement behind the protagonist.
Dialogue: "Who's there?"
SFX: none
Balloon/caption placement: Bubble in open pavement area.

Panel 5:
Draw: Dramatic reaction close-up, protagonist turning around with alarm.
Dialogue: "Answer me!"
SFX: "バッ"
Balloon/caption placement: Dialogue bubble upper right, SFX near shoulder motion line.
```

Keep panel briefs short enough to fit in a single image prompt, but specific enough that each panel has a distinct camera, action, and text placement.
